Madhav Jolly

Resume
Designer — Brand & Digital Product, Melbourne

Madhav Jolly is an Indian designer in Melbourne, working across brand and digital product. Identity first, then the system around it — with motion, 3D, and sound when the work needs more than a still. Useful, then a little strange on purpose.

Outside of design, he produces electronic music, digs for rare records, reads sci-fi, solves cryptic crosswords, and spends time with his dog.

Embracing the challenge

I enjoy stepping into the unfamiliar and finding clarity in complexity. I'm drawn to messy briefs, shifting constraints, and open-ended ideas. I see the real energy in exploring and refining, turning uncertainty into focused, intentional work.

Crafting with magic

I work across identity and digital design, with a focus on building systems that feel both clear and alive. I often bring in motion, 3D, and sonic elements to add depth and personality, shaping ideas into experiences that feel intuitive, expressive, and easy to engage with.

Play & experimentation

Outside of client work, I like staying close to curiosity. I make electronic music, experiment with code, illustrate often, and explore different creative mediums. These side explorations keep my practice open, playful, and constantly evolving.
