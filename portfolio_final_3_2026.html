About

I'm Madhav, a designer in Melbourne working across brand and digital product. Graphic design in India, then an M.Des in Interaction Design at Monash. 3+ years in agencies, plus freelance work alongside my Master's: identities, packaging, motion, and products from a first sketch through to launch.

I work across creative direction, visual systems, and digital product, with motion, 3D, and sound when a still is not enough.
